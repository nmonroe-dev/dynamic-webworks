05 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before we begin.

Are these real client projects?+

These 15 projects are original concept builds created to show design direction, website sections and possible business uses. They are not claims of client relationships, live businesses or measured results.

What is included in the price?+

Each estimate covers the website scope described on its project page. Final pricing follows a written scope. Domain registration, hosting, paid subscriptions, transaction fees, photography and ongoing maintenance are separate unless expressly included.

Can customers book, shop or request a quote?+

The concepts demonstrate those journeys. A real launch can connect an appropriate booking, payment or form provider. Provider fees and setup scope are confirmed before work begins. The sample interfaces do not submit appointments or process payments.

How long does a website take?+

Timing depends on the agreed scope and how quickly content and feedback are supplied. We establish milestones after discovery and confirm a schedule in the proposal.
