THE BUSINESS

About this business

Form Athletics is a sample fitness trainer / gym focused on small-group training, personal coaching and structured fitness programs. Its website is designed for new members who want a welcoming starting point and a program that fits their schedule. The concept starts with those customer needs and gives each section a specific role in the decision.

What this website includes

The experience brings together program finder, membership plans, class schedule, coach profiles, trial request, training faq. A #d1884f accent palette, industry-specific imagery and a dramatic dark visual direction build a recognizable identity. Clear navigation connects discovery, consideration and action.

These are illustrative website designs and proposed workflows. Booking, payments, live inventory, maps and submissions require the appropriate provider and configuration in a real client project. No customer data is collected by the section previews.

The homepage hero

Program finder

The program finder presents small-group training, personal coaching and structured fitness programs in a clear, approachable format. The design groups related information into short blocks so visitors can compare options and understand the business without reading a wall of marketing copy.

How it helps the business

Form Athletics needs to serve new members who want a welcoming starting point and a program that fits their schedule. This section addresses that decision with relevant detail, a consistent visual hierarchy and a visible path to find your first session.

A better customer experience

People can quickly find the information that matters to them and decide whether to continue. Descriptive headings and readable spacing make the page useful on a phone as well as on a larger screen.

Business value

06 / Trial request · Explore the concept · Visual illustration

06 / THE EXPERIENCE

Trial request

This trial request gives visitors a structured way to express interest in small-group training, personal coaching and structured fitness programs. It collects the relevant service, timing and contact details in a short sequence instead of asking customers to compose an unstructured message.

How it helps the business

For Form Athletics, a better brief means fewer clarification emails and a more useful first conversation. The final scope would define the required fields and connect a suitable provider; this preview does not confirm availability or submit requests.

A better customer experience

Visitors can see what information is needed before they begin. Clear labels, helpful next-step language and a mobile-friendly layout reduce uncertainty, especially when someone is comparing several businesses.

Business value

The potential benefit is fewer abandoned inquiries and less administrative back-and-forth. A production launch would track completed requests and response times to judge whether the journey is working.

07 / Training FAQ · Explore the concept · Visual illustration

07 / THE EXPERIENCE

Training FAQ

The training faq answers the practical questions that can stall a decision about small-group training, personal coaching and structured fitness programs. It turns specialist knowledge into a readable sequence with clear headings and a next step for visitors who need personal guidance.

How it helps the business

For Form Athletics, this creates a consistent explanation that customers can revisit. Staff spend less time repeating introductory information and more time discussing the details that are specific to each customer.

A better customer experience

People can prepare at their own pace and understand what to expect before reaching out. Accessible text and concise answers help mobile visitors find an answer without downloading documents or navigating several unrelated pages.

Business value

The potential value is better-prepared customers and fewer repetitive questions. Content would be reviewed by the business, and regulated or specialist information would require approval by an appropriately qualified professional.
