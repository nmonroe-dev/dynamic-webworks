DYNAMIC WEBWORKS — WEBSITE FILES

QUICK PREVIEW ON WINDOWS
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or your browser.
3. All website pages, 15 portfolio showcases, images and interactive demos are included.
   Keep the assets and portfolio folders next to index.html.
4. Optional: Open this folder in VS Code. You can use Live Server, but you do not need it to preview the static pages.

TO PUBLISH
Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (not the ZIP file) to the public directory of a static host.
Examples: Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, GitHub Pages, a traditional cPanel public_html directory.
Use index.html as the homepage. All links are file-relative and work locally and on standard static hosting.

IMPORTANT BEFORE SENDING TO CUSTOMERS
- The contact form prepares an email to dynamicwebworksleads@gmail.com in the visitor's email app.
  They must SEND that email; the site does not silently submit leads.
  For a true website form, connect a form provider later.
- Stripe checkout/payment links are NOT connected. Payments are handled personally after a quote.
- The portfolio pages are illustrative CONCEPTS and sample interfaces; no live appointment,
  database, store checkout or real bookings are performed.
- Edit your published terms, privacy information, legal identity, and payment policies to reflect
  actual business operations before launch.
- Domain, hosting, SSL, and recurring provider costs depend on the chosen platform.
